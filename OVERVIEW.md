## 1. Executive Summary

**Ocean Abundance Corporation** is a Singapore-headquartered ocean ranching company that deploys a fleet of 12 self-sustaining, storm-proof, semi-submersible platforms ("Tiger Leviathan-class") to breed, release, and harvest giant tiger prawns (*Penaeus monodon*) at planetary scale.

Each 260m platform is a complete biological factory:
- Houses **200,000 broodstock females** producing **300 billion juveniles per vessel per year** (3.6 trillion fleet-wide)

### The Core Problem

The ocean covers 71% of the planet but provides only 17% of human protein. Wild-catch fisheries are maxed out -- 90% of stocks are fully or over-exploited. Traditional aquaculture is constrained by:

- **Land**: Coastal pond aquaculture destroys mangroves and competes with agriculture
- **Water**: Freshwater aquaculture depletes aquifers
- **Feed**: Fishmeal-based feed requires wild-caught fish (FIFO ratio >1.0)
- **Disease**: High-density net-pen operations suffer disease outbreaks (EMS, AHPND, WSSV)
- **Antibiotics**: Prophylactic antibiotic use creates resistance
- **Location**: Fixed operations cannot follow optimal temperatures
- **Scale**: Land-based systems cannot achieve the volume needed to impact global food security

### The Gap

No existing approach can produce protein at the scale, sustainability, and cost needed to meaningfully address global hunger. The gap between what is needed (billions of meals) and what is produced (millions of tonnes) requires a fundamentally new approach.

### 5.2 Recapture Technology

**Baseline recapture**: 35% (with no conditioning, ocean ranching of shrimp)

**Combined boost**: +30 percentage points over baseline

**Benchmark**: Salmon ocean ranching achieves ~4% recapture. Our 65% target is ambitious but achievable with acoustic + pheromone conditioning, larger release size (7.5g vs 0.1g for salmon), predator saturation (continuous trickle release overwhelms local predators), and the drone-herding spread that physically walks the school into the net.

- Allows verification that recaptured shrimp are our released stock (not wild)
- Supports 48-hour global recall capability
